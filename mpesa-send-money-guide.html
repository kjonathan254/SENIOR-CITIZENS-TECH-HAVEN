How to Send Money via M-Pesa for Beginners (Using Hakikisha Safely)

By Kevin Jonathan Otieno | Updated August 4, 2026 | 8 min read

Quick Summary: Sending money via M-Pesa is safe and easy when you follow one golden rule: ALWAYS use Hakikisha. This free service shows you the recipient's name BEFORE you confirm. If the name is wrong, press Cancel. Nothing bad happens. This guide walks you through every step.

Why M-Pesa is Safe for Seniors (When Done Right)

M-Pesa has revolutionized how Kenyans send money, and it's perfectly safe for older adults when you know the right steps. Millions of seniors use it daily to send money to family, pay bills, and shop.

The secret? Hakikisha (which means "to make sure" in Kiswahili). This feature was designed specifically to prevent mistakes like sending money to the wrong number.

🛡️ The Hakikisha Promise

Before you enter your PIN, Safaricom WILL show you the recipient's name.

If you meant to send to "Mary Wanjiku" but the screen says "John Kamau" — STOP. Press Cancel. Try again. Your money stays safe in your account until YOU confirm.

Step-by-Step: Sending Money via M-Pesa

Follow these 6 simple steps every time you send money. Take your time — there's no rush.

Step 1: Go to M-Pesa Menu

On your phone, go to the main menu and select M-Pesa. You'll be asked for your M-Pesa PIN. Enter it carefully, shielding the keypad with your hand.

Step 2: Select "Send Money"

From the M-Pesa menu, choose "Send Money" (usually option 1). This is for sending to another phone number.

Step 3: Enter the Phone Number

Type the recipient's phone number carefully. Double-check each digit. If you have them saved in your contacts, you can select from there instead of typing.

Step 4: Enter the Amount

Type the amount you want to send (e.g., 500, 1000, 2000). Make sure you have enough balance including the small transaction fee.

Step 5: WAIT for Hakikisha Screen

This is the most important step! Before you enter your PIN, a screen will appear showing:

  • The phone number
  • The recipient's registered name (e.g., "MARY WANJIKU")
  • The amount

READ THE NAME SLOWLY. Is this the person you meant to send to?

  • ✅ If YES: Proceed to Step 6
  • ❌ If NO: Press "Cancel" immediately. Your money is still safe. Start over.
Step 6: Enter PIN and Confirm

Only after confirming the name is correct, enter your M-Pesa PIN. You'll receive a confirmation SMS shortly.

⚠️ Critical Safety Rules

🚫 NEVER Share Your PIN

Your M-Pesa PIN is yours alone. Safaricom will NEVER ask for it via call, SMS, or WhatsApp. Anyone who asks for your PIN is a scammer trying to steal your money.

  • Don't tell it to anyone on the phone
  • Don't write it down in your phone book
  • Don't use obvious numbers like 1234 or your birth year
  • Shield the keypad when entering it in public

💡 Pro Tips for Success

  • Save frequent contacts: Store family members' numbers in your phone so you don't have to type them each time.
  • Call first for large amounts: Before sending KSh 5,000 or more, call the person to confirm their number.
  • Keep confirmation SMS: Don't delete M-Pesa messages until you're sure the transaction was correct. They're your receipt.
  • Use slow fingers: Rushing causes mistakes. Take a deep breath and go step-by-step.

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Mistake How to Prevent It
Typing wrong number Save contacts in phone; call to verify before sending
Not checking Hakikisha name Make it a habit: ALWAYS read the name before PIN
Rushing the process Take your time; there's no deadline to send money
Sharing PIN with "helpers" Never let anyone else enter your PIN, even if they're "helping"
Deleting confirmation SMS Keep all M-Pesa messages as proof of transaction

What If You Make a Mistake?

Even with Hakikisha, mistakes can happen. If you sent money to the wrong number:

  1. Don't panic. Act quickly but calmly.
  2. Forward the confirmation SMS to 456 (free) within 24 hours.
  3. Or dial *234# → My Account → Reverse Transaction.
  4. Call 100 if you need human help.

For detailed reversal instructions, read our full guide: How to Reverse an M-Pesa Transaction Sent to the Wrong Number.

Sending to Other Networks (Airtel, Telkom)

You can also send money from M-Pesa to Airtel Money or Telkom Pochi La Jumia accounts. The process is similar:

  1. Select "Send Money" from M-Pesa menu
  2. Enter the phone number (any network)
  3. Enter amount
  4. Hakikisha will still show the name
  5. Confirm with PIN

The recipient will receive the money in their mobile wallet regardless of their network.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hakikisha? ▼
Hakikisha is a free Safaricom service that displays the recipient's registered name before you confirm an M-Pesa transaction. It's designed to prevent sending money to the wrong number. Always wait for this screen and verify the name matches who you intend to pay.
Is M-Pesa safe for seniors? ▼
Yes, M-Pesa is very safe when you follow key rules: always use Hakikisha to verify names, never share your PIN with anyone, double-check numbers before sending, and keep your confirmation SMS messages. Millions of Kenyan seniors use M-Pesa safely every day.
What if I send money to the wrong number? ▼
Act fast! Forward the confirmation SMS to 456 within 24 hours (free), or dial *234# and select "Reverse Transaction". If it's been longer or the money was withdrawn, call Safaricom on 100 immediately. Read our full reversal guide for detailed steps.
Can I send M-Pesa to Airtel or Telkom? ▼
Yes! You can send money from M-Pesa to any phone number regardless of network. The recipient will receive it in their mobile wallet (Airtel Money or Telkom Pochi La Jumia). The Hakikisha feature works for all networks.
What if Hakikisha doesn't show a name? ▼
If Hakikisha doesn't display a name, the number may not be registered with Safaricom or may be new. In this case, call the recipient first to confirm the number is correct before proceeding. Never send money without verifying.

Real Story: How Mama Wanjiku Avoided a KSh 10,000 Mistake

Mama Wanjiku from Nakuru wanted to send KSh 10,000 to her daughter. She typed what she thought was her daughter's number. When the Hakikisha screen appeared, it showed "JOHN KAMAU" instead of "WANGUI MWANIKI."

"I almost cried," she recalls. "I had typed one digit wrong. But because I waited for the name, I caught it. I pressed Cancel, checked my contacts, and sent it correctly. Hakikisha saved my money!"

This happens every day. Hakikisha is your friend. Use it.
