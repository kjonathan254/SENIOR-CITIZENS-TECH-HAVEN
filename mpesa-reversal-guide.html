How to Reverse an M-Pesa Transaction

A step-by-step guide for older adults in Kenya

✍️ Kevin Jonathan Otieno 📅 July 2026 ⏱️ ~6 min read 💰 Finance & M-Pesa

Quick Summary

If you sent money to the wrong number, do not panic. You have two ways to reverse it: dial *456# and follow the prompts, or forward the original M-Pesa SMS to 456. Do not send another payment. Do not share your PIN with anyone — not even someone claiming to be from Safaricom.
